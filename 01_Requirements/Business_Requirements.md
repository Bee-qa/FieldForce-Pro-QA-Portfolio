## 2. Purpose 
|--------|---------|
| Australian BG Engineering Pty Ltd (ABG) currently manages field operations using spreadsheets, paper forms, emails and phone calls. These manual processes create delays, increase the risk of data errors, reduce visibility into field activities and require significant administrative effort. 

The purpose of the FieldForce Pro application is to provide a centralized web-based platform that enables field engineers, supervisors and office staff to efficiently manage jobs, attendance, leave requests, timesheets and operational reporting from a single system. |

### 3. Business Objectives
|--------|---------|
The FieldForce Pro project aims to achieve the following business objectives:

- Replace paper-based and spreadsheet-driven workflows with a centralized digital solution.
- Reduce manual administrative effort for office staff.
- Improve the accuracy of attendance, timesheet and payroll records.
- Enable field engineers to update job information in real time.
- Improve communication between office staff, supervisors and field engineers.
- Provide management with real-time operational dashboards and reports.
- Reduce duplicate data entry and human errors.
