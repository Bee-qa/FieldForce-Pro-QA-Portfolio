# Requirements Traceability Matrix (RTM)

## Purpose

The Requirements Traceability Matrix maps business and functional requirements to user stories, test scenarios, and test cases.

It provides end-to-end traceability to confirm that defined requirements are covered by appropriate testing activities and helps identify potential gaps in test coverage.
